PROJECT REPORT GUIDELINES 
PART A: GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS 
Formatting Requirements (Mandatory) 
 Font: Times New Roman 
 Font Size: 
o Headings: 14–16 pt (Bold) 
o Body Text: 12 pt 
 Line Spacing: 1.5 
 Margins: 1 inch on all sides 
 Alignment: Justified 
 Page Numbering: Bottom center 
 Paper Size: A4 
 Binding: Hard bound / spiral bound 
 Submission Format: Printed copy + PDF 
 
Report Length 
 Minimum: 50 pages 
 Recommended: 60–80 pages 
 Excluding: Certificates, references, appendix 
 
Writing Standards 
 Use formal academic language 
 Avoid grammatical errors and informal tone 
 Maintain logical flow and coherence 
 Avoid plagiarism (strict action if detected) 
 Include figures, tables, graphs, and diagrams wherever applicable 
 
Mandatory Components of a Project 
Every report must clearly demonstrate: 
 
 Problem definition 
 Literature support 
 Methodology/design 
 Implementation 
 Results and analysis 
 Conclusion and future work
